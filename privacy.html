Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Pace is operated by Johannes Rampp, contact: support@pace-online.de.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Pace collects, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and what rights you have. We only process the data needed to run the service, and we never sell your data.

/ 1. Data we collect

  • Account data — your email address and a password (passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile data — display name, age, gender, bio, photos, city, chosen sports and levels, and answers to profile prompts.
  • Location data — precise GPS coordinates or the city/postal code you pick. Coordinates are stored in a private, protected table and are never shown to other users; other users only see a coarse approximate distance calculated from a privacy-protected location.
  • Activity data — your swipes, matches, and chat messages with matched athletes.
  • Purchase data — which premium tier you purchased and the store transaction identifiers (product and transaction ID), validated against the Apple App Store or Google Play.

/ 2. How we use your data

  • Show you nearby athletes and compute distances, applying your match preferences.
  • Create matches and enable chat between matched users.
  • Grant and manage premium entitlements based on valid store purchases.
  • Keep the service safe — detect abuse, spam and fraudulent purchases.

/ 3. Legal basis

We process personal data on the following bases under the GDPR: to perform the contract you enter into by using Pace (Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and operational (Art. 6(1)(f)); consent where we ask for it (Art. 6(1)(a)); and compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

/ 4. Sharing

  • Supabase — hosts our database and photo storage; data is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Apple / Google — process payments and subscriptions; purchase validation is performed through their official APIs.
  • Open-Meteo — when you type a city or postal code, that text query is sent to open-meteo.com to return matching places. No profile data is shared.

We do not show ads, do not sell personal data, and use no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

/ 5. Retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Edit profile → Danger zone → Delete account) or from your browser at delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: profile, photos, swipes, matches, messages, location and premium entitlements are removed.

If you have a paid subscription, it continues until cancelled in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings — deleting the Pace account does not stop store billing.

/ 6. Children

Pace is only available to people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

/ 7. Security

We use HTTPS, row-level security on the database, signed, time-limited URLs for photos, and server-side validation of store receipts. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow industry-standard practices.

/ 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased (including by deleting your account);
  • restrict or object to processing in certain circumstances;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@pace-online.de.

/ 9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision; significant changes will be communicated in the app.
